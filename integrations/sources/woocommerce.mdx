WooCommerce is live as a Tranthor commerce data source. Use it to connect a WooCommerce store so Tranthor can work with the store data behind your campaigns.

This page only describes what the public product pages and the existing docs support. Setup details that are not in this repository, and that are not in product code visible while writing this page, are marked **TODO (owner)** instead of guessed.

## How it works

WooCommerce is listed next to Shopify as a commerce source. After you connect a store, Tranthor can use that store's commerce data in your workspace.

## Troubleshooting

If people or orders do not show up after you connect:

- Confirm the WooCommerce source shows as connected in **Settings**.
- Confirm Tranthor is actually receiving data. The [frequently asked questions](https://docs.tranthor.com/guide/faq) page says users stay missing when no user events have been sent yet, or when your properties and segments do not match the events you send.
